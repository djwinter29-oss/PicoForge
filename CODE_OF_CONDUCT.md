# Code of Conduct

PicoForge is a collaborative project. Participants in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces are expected to help keep them respectful, useful, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful when discussing technical disagreements.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack or demean contributors.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback and correction.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or private communications without permission.
- Keep feedback relevant to the project and make participation accessible to people with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. This applies to project spaces and to conduct outside them when it materially affects a participant's safety or ability to contribute.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately through the repository's GitHub reporting/contact controls or directly to a project maintainer. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Include relevant links or context and share only information needed to understand the concern.

Project maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible, protect reporter confidentiality to the extent practical, and may edit or remove content or limit participation when needed. Maintainers should explain decisions to affected parties where safe and appropriate. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact another repository owner or the hosting platform instead.

This policy applies to all project-managed communication spaces and contribution processes.
