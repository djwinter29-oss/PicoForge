Save and reboot?

Firmware saves boot settings, then reboots the board and briefly disconnects USB. Close all CLI ports and stop external traffic first. Stop any storage test or USB drive activity before saving.

Your computer owns the SD card. Rebooting disconnects the drive; cached host writes may be lost and the filesystem may be corrupted. Safely eject first. Confirming forces USB detach before saving and rebooting. A successful device sync does not flush your computer's cached writes.

Reboot board?

This immediately reboots the board and briefly disconnects USB. Close all CLI ports and stop external traffic first.

Enable USB drive?

Pico will unmount its filesystem and give your computer exclusive access to the SD card. Updated firmware keeps HID and the serial console connected. Self-test is unavailable while shared; Save and Reboot requires a data-loss confirmation on supporting firmware. Safely eject the drive on your computer before returning storage to Pico.

Return storage to Pico?

Cached host writes may be lost and the filesystem may be corrupted. Stop all host file activity first. Pico will revoke host access to the card and attempt to remount without formatting or repair.
