self-test: unavailable

Save and reboot?

Firmware saves boot settings, then reboots the board and briefly disconnects USB. Close all CLI ports and stop external traffic first. Stop any storage test or USB drive activity before saving.

Reboot board?

This immediately reboots the board and briefly disconnects USB. Close all CLI ports and stop external traffic first.

Enable USB drive?

Pico will unmount its filesystem and give your computer exclusive access to the SD card. USB briefly reconnects. Self-test and Save and Reboot are unavailable while the drive is shared. Safely eject the drive on your computer before returning storage to Pico.

Return storage to Pico?

Cached host writes may be lost and the filesystem may be corrupted. Stop all host file activity first and safely eject whenever possible. Pico will detach the USB drive and attempt to remount without formatting or repair.
